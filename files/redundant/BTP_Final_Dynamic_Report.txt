Arousal: Agreement = 54.2%, Fleiss' Kappa (IAA) = 0.043
INFERENCE: Participants broadly agree on the emotional category (high %), but the low Kappa suggests high individual subjectivity in specific ratings.

Q2. Self-Reported Induced Match vs. Perception Alignment

Arousal: 100.0% (p=e group) vs 100.0% (p!=e group) | Chi-Sq p-val: 1.0000
INFERENCE: Self-reports show near-identical results regardless of understanding, suggesting a 'Cognitive Expectation Bias' in user surveys.

Q3. Physiologically Inferred Induced Match vs. Perception Alignment

Arousal: Correlation = 0.106, Cohen's Kappa = 0.064
INFERENCE: Low correlation proves Mind and Body are non-redundant. Physiological signals capture 'Hidden Drift' that conscious reporting misses.
